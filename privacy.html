Privacy Policy

Home Secretary · Last updated: 7 November 2026

Who uses this app

Home Secretary is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google account. No other people's accounts are connected.

Google user data the app accesses

  • Gmail: email messages and labels, to summarize mail and prepare drafts.
  • Google Calendar: events, to show the schedule and propose changes.
  • Google Tasks: task lists, to include open tasks in summaries.
  • Google Drive and Contacts: read-only, to look up files and names the owner asks about.
  • Basic profile: name and email address, to identify the connected account.

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide the features above to the owner. Actions that change data, such as sending an email or changing an event, are performed only after the owner approves each one.

Storage

Data and access tokens are stored only on the owner's home computer. They are not uploaded to any server operated by the app. Processing is done by a language model running on that same computer.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data and revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's folder on the home computer removes all stored data and tokens.
