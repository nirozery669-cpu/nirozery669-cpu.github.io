Home Secretary

A private, single-user assistant that runs on its owner's home computer.

What it does

  • Reads the owner's Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Tasks to prepare summaries and a daily morning briefing.
  • Prepares draft replies and proposed calendar changes for the owner to review.
  • Uses a language model that runs locally on the owner's computer.

Who can use it

This app is for the personal use of its owner only. It is not offered to the public and does not accept sign-ups.
